---
name: opportunity-hunter
description: Discover and rank concrete B2B business opportunities based on recurring expensive problems; use for idea generation and opportunity screening.
---
Identify 15 specific user problems with actor, frequency, workaround, cost, trigger and buying authority. Prefer France B2B, accounting firms and SMEs, but compare other niches. Do not invent demand. Score pain 25, willingness to pay 25, distribution 15, differentiation 15, MVP feasibility 10, retention 10. Mark unknowns. Shortlist top 3 only for deep research.
